# Research Initiation Report

Came here to post [my FCTUC report](https://matias-math.github.io/public-files/FCTUCEnglish.pdf) on the research initiation grant.
I forgot to have it posted here before! My bad . . . :)

The report is quite small, FCTUC gave us a rather small maximum number of pages. But I feel like I was able to put my result in there.

It gives a partial answer to a really fun problem, which fits in both categories: Packing and Optimization Problems.

I believe We can still get a better result on the symmetry of the solution! But, for that, we would need to find out if there is any kind of offset angle between the centers of the source balls and the center of the domain!

I really recommend checking out the moving plane method in Fanghua's book. It can be easily played around with to get a variety of moving plane methods, which might be used to solve a lot of optimization problems.
